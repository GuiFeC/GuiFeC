## <img src="https://cdn.simpleicons.org/googlescholar" width="22" height="22" /> About Me

I'm an **Information Systems undergraduate at the Federal University of Acre (UFAC)**, interested in applying computing and data-driven approaches to real-world social and economic problems.

My academic work combines **Data Science, Artificial Intelligence and Software Engineering** with interdisciplinary research in areas such as **HealthTech, Food and Nutrition Security, tourism, public policy and regional development in the Brazilian Amazon**.

I also have experience in Full-Stack development with **Java, Spring Boot, Angular and Node.js**, as well as participation in research projects, hackathons and Game Jams.
